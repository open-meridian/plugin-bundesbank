# Terms

A summary, not legal advice: read the Bundesbank's own words before relying
on them. Terms change; these were read on 19 November 2026.

From the Bundesbank's page on the reuse of ESCB statistics
([bundesbank.de](https://www.bundesbank.de/en/homepage/user-information/terms-of-use-regarding-the-reuse-of-escb-statistics-621188)):

- **Free reuse.** The ESCB's statistics are free of charge and may be
  reused whether the use is commercial or not.
- **Quoting the source.** Reuse credits the source; the plugin's Datasets
  page carries "Source: Deutsche Bundesbank".
- **Unmodified.** The statistics, metadata included, are not modified. The
  plugin records each value as the Bundesbank writes it.
- **Third-party data** are not covered without the originator's prior
  permission. The term structure is the Bundesbank's own estimate.
- **No continuity.** No statistic is promised to continue; figures may be
  revised, and access may be restricted in exceptional cases.

The dataset describes these terms in words, shown on the Data sources page,
and declares no licence. The licence a deployment admin sets there is the
one enforced; the plugin says nothing of whether your deployment meets
anyone's terms.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Deutsche Bundesbank.
